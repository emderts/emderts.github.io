Trait · Long Howl → 긴 울부짖음
If any effect triggers Long Howl, Spawn the White Ape Troll in the center of the Clash Board (Displace any miniatures if needed), then turn it to face the most Knights. If this effect would trigger when the Ape Troll is already on the Clash Board, ignore it. The Ape Troll uses Firstman Warrior attributes. Look for BP 2 Muscular Chest and AI 2 Rampage Strike from the Ape Troll’s decks and place them revealed next to the Clash Board. Never discard or Escalate these cards. The Ape Troll activates after Firstmen Warriors and performs Rampage Strike. When you attack the Ape Troll, you always target the Muscular Chest BP. When you wound that BP, Escalate the Firstmen Warriors AI deck, then place a Generic token on the BP and place a Generic Single Wound card on the Wound Stack. If there are 4 Generic tokens on the BP, the White Ape Troll will flee from the Clash.
